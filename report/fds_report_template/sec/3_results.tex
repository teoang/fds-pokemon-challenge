\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\begin{table}[h!]
  \caption{CV accuracy (5-fold $\times$ 3) and paired gain over the baseline ($\pm$ corrected SE).}
  \label{tab:results}
  \centering
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
    \toprule
    Configuration & CV acc. & Gain \\
    \midrule
    State at face value & 0.675 & $-0.010$ \\
    Baseline (+ species, items, moves) & 0.685 & -- \\
    Final without hidden knockouts & 0.687 & $+0.002$ \\
    Final without P1 history & 0.697 & $+0.012$ \\
    \textbf{Final model} & \textbf{0.699} & $\mathbf{+0.014\pm0.004}$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Table~\ref{tab:results} summarises the ablation. Almost all of the gain comes from the hidden-knockout
correction ($+0.012\pm0.003$); the history adds $+0.002\pm0.004$ once species are present.
It scored 0.700 on the public and 0.683 on the private leaderboard. The private score is 1.5 points
below CV (two standard errors of a 3,500-battle split), and our best submissions lie within noise
of each other there. Code:
\url{https://github.com/teoang/fds-pokemon-challenge}.
