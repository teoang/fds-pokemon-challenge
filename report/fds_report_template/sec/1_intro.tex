\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The challenge gives 10,000 labelled and 5,000 test battles. For each battle we see Player~1's full
team, Player~2's lead and the first 30 recorded turns (active Pok\'emon, HP, status, boosts, moves);
the target is whether Player~1 wins, and the metric is accuracy. Battles last 30 to 80 turns, so
the outcome is still open when the log ends.

Two facts shaped our solution. First, Player~2's \texttt{fnt} state appears almost only in double knockouts or at the
very last record: about one Player~2 knockout per battle is hidden. Second, the same Player~1 team
recurs across battles, and its win rate elsewhere predicts the outcome, while Player~2's does not.
