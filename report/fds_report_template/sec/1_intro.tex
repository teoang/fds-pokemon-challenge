\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

We are given 10,000 labelled and 5,000 test Gen~2 OU battles. From Player~1's team, Player~2's lead and the
first 30 recorded turns we must say whether Player~1 wins.
Battles last 30 to 80 turns, so the log stops while the outcome is still open.

What does the log really show? Two facts emerged. Player~2's
\texttt{fnt} state appears almost only in double knockouts: about one Player~2 knockout per battle
looks like an ordinary switch. And the same Player~1 team recurs across battles, with a win rate
that predicts the outcome, while Player~2's does not. How can we turn these facts into features?
